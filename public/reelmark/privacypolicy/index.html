Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 6, 2026

Here's the deal

We want to be upfront with you - ReelMark doesn't store your data. Everything you save stays right on your phone, and that's exactly how we designed it.

How it all works

When you save a reel, your phone grabs the video directly from Instagram and keeps it on your device. We built ReelMark to help make that happen smoothly, but the videos and your collection? That's all yours, stored locally on your phone.

What stays with you

Your saved reels, your download history, your whole collection - it all lives on your device. There's no account to create, no cloud storage on our end, and no way for us to peek at what you've saved. It's your personal collection.

Sharing with others

Since we don't hold onto any of your information, there's nothing for us to share with anyone else. When you use ReelMark, your phone connects to Instagram to fetch the video - and that's the only external connection involved.

Your space, your rules

Because everything stays on your device, you're in control. Want to delete something? Just remove it from your phone. Your privacy is built into how the app works.

Keeping you updated

If we ever make changes to how things work, we'll update this page so you know what's up. We like keeping things simple and transparent.

Got questions?

We're happy to help if anything's unclear. Feel free to reach out anytime - we love hearing from people who use ReelMark!
