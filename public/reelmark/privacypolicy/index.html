Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Here's the deal

ReelMark is local-first. Your saved reels, notes, takeaways and to-dos live on your device, and there's no account or sign-in. The AI features and video playback do need to send some things elsewhere, so here's exactly what leaves your phone, where it goes, and why.

What stays with you

Your collection, your notes, your takeaways and your to-dos are stored on your device. They only leave it when you use an AI feature, as described below.

Previews

To show a preview of a reel, your phone reads the reel's public page on the site it came from, such as Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, for its title, description and thumbnail.

AI Analysis

When you run AI Analysis, the reel's link, its description and your notes go to our server at reels.krylabs.com. Our server downloads the reel's public video, transcribes it, and sends the text to an AI provider, currently Groq, to generate the results.

Chat, summaries and script reports

These include the reel's title, description, transcript and your notes. On iPhone, version 3.1 and earlier send them straight from your phone to Google's Gemini API and Groq. On Android and later iPhone versions, they go through our server to an AI provider, currently Groq, along with an anonymous device ID, a random identifier that isn't tied to who you are. Our server passes those messages along and doesn't save them.

What our server keeps

Our server keeps the video, the reel's public details (its link, the creator's username, the caption and its like, view and comment counts), the transcript, the analysis and any script report, so the same reel isn't processed twice. Playing or saving a reel's video in the app fetches it from this copy. These records are stored by reel, with no account or device ID attached.

Your notes and AI Analysis

Your notes help shape the analysis, so it can reflect what you wrote, and that analysis is reused when someone else analyzes the same reel. Please keep personal details out of your notes if you use AI Analysis.

Technical details

Our server's logs record requests, including which reel links were asked for. For Chat through our server they also hold a short piece of the device ID and the size of each request, but not your messages. These logs rotate automatically. Requests to our server pass through Cloudflare, which handles your IP address to deliver them.

Purchases

ReelMark Premium is billed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Sharing with others

We don't sell your data. Beyond the AI providers (Google and Groq) and Cloudflare described above, nothing you send us goes to anyone else.

Your space, your rules

Delete something from the app and it's gone from the app. Videos you saved to your photo library or gallery stay there until you delete them yourself. If you'd like a reel's copy removed from our server, email us and we'll take it down.

Keeping you updated

If we ever make changes to how things work, we'll update this page so you know what's up. We like keeping things simple and transparent.
