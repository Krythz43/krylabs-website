Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Here's the deal

ReelMark is local-first. Your saved reels, notes, takeaways and to-dos live on your device, and there's no account or sign-in. A few features do need our server, so here's exactly what leaves your phone, and why.

What stays with you

Your collection, your notes, your takeaways and your to-dos are stored on your device. We can't see them unless you send them to an AI feature, as described below.

Previews

To show a preview of a reel, your phone reads the reel's public page on Instagram for its title, description and thumbnail.

What goes to our server

When you run AI Analysis or Chat, the reel's link and related text (its description, your notes and its transcript) go to our server at reels.krylabs.com. Our server forwards that text to an AI provider, currently Groq, to generate the results. Playing or saving a reel's video in the app also fetches the video from our server.

What our server keeps

To analyze a reel, our server downloads the public video from Instagram and transcribes it. It keeps the video, the reel's public details (its link, the creator's username, the caption and its like, view and comment counts), the transcript and the analysis, so the same reel isn't processed twice. These records are about the reel, not about you: they aren't linked to you, your device or an account. Chat messages are passed along to generate a reply and aren't saved by our server.

Technical details

Chat sends an anonymous device ID, a random identifier that isn't tied to who you are. Our logs record a short piece of it, along with the size of each request and how long it took, but not your messages, and those logs rotate automatically. Requests to our server pass through Cloudflare, which handles your IP address to deliver them.

Purchases

ReelMark Premium is billed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Sharing with others

We don't sell your data. Beyond the AI provider and Cloudflare described above, nothing you send us goes to anyone else.

Your space, your rules

Delete something from the app and it's gone from your device. If you'd like a reel's copy removed from our server, email us and we'll take it down.

Keeping you updated

If we ever make changes to how things work, we'll update this page so you know what's up. We like keeping things simple and transparent.
