What BlockBud Is

BlockBud is an iOS app that helps you manage screen time by:

  • Blocking apps and websites you choose
  • Creating customizable "shield screens" when blocked apps are opened

It uses Apple's Screen Time API to do all this. The app is designed for personal use to help YOU manage YOUR screen time on YOUR device.
