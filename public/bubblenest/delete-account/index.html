Delete Your BubbleNest Account

This page applies to the BubbleNest app by KryLabs.

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. When your account is deleted, we remove all data associated with it from our servers, including:

  • Your profile (name, age, interests, preferences, and profile details)
  • Your photos (removed from our servers; nothing is retained after deletion)
  • Your matches, chats, and messages
  • Your bubbles, comments, and community memberships
  • Your connection requests, vibe tokens, and event preferences
  • Your device identifiers used for push notifications

No data is retained after account deletion, as described in our Privacy Policy. Active subscriptions are managed by Google Play or the App Store — cancel them in your store account, as deleting your BubbleNest account does not cancel a store subscription.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open BubbleNest and go to your Profile
  2. Open Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Option 2 — Delete right here

Sign in with the same Google account you use in BubbleNest, and we will permanently delete your account and all its data immediately.
