Child Safety Standards

BubbleNest: Friend Finding App, by KryLabs · Last updated: July 4, 2026

These are BubbleNest's published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We have zero tolerance for any content or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers children — anywhere on our service, in any form. This applies to real, fictional, illustrated, AI-generated, and text-based material alike.

1. BubbleNest is an adults-only service

  • BubbleNest is strictly for users aged 18 and over. Onboarding requires a date-of-birth declaration, and the app refuses to create a profile for anyone under 18.
  • Accounts we identify or reasonably suspect as belonging to a person under 18 are removed.
  • An adults-only policy does not lessen our obligations: CSAE and CSAM are prohibited absolutely, including content depicting minors and adults soliciting or sharing such content.

2. Prohibited content and behavior

We prohibit, without exception:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind — sharing, requesting, linking to, or describing it.
  • Grooming: adults building relationships with minors for sexual purposes, on or off the platform.
  • Sexualization of minors, including sexualized commentary, imagery, or role-play involving minors.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, and any facilitation of child sexual exploitation.
  • Attempts by minors to access the service by misstating their age.

3. In-app reporting and moderation

  • Any user can be reported directly in the app — from their profile, their posts ("bubbles"), or a chat — with categories including inappropriate content and harassment. Users can also block anyone instantly.
  • Child-safety concerns can also be reported to us by email at any time (see contact below); you do not need an account to report.
  • Our team reviews reports promptly. Content that violates these standards is removed and the offending account is terminated. We err on the side of removal.

4. Enforcement and reporting to authorities

  • When we become aware of apparent CSAM, we remove it, terminate the account, and preserve relevant material as evidence for law enforcement rather than merely deleting it.
  • We report apparent CSAM and child endangerment to the relevant regional and national authorities — including law enforcement in the user's jurisdiction and, where applicable, mechanisms such as the NCMEC CyberTipline — and we cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement.
  • We comply with all child safety laws applicable in the regions where BubbleNest operates.

5. Child safety point of contact

Our designated child-safety contact is prepared to discuss BubbleNest's CSAM prevention practices and compliance with these standards:

Email: info.krylabs@gmail.com
Please use the subject line "Child safety report" for urgent reports. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local law enforcement first.

See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. These standards apply to the BubbleNest apps on Android and iOS and to all connected services.
