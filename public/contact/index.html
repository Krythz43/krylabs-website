Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?

While logged into the app, go to the Terms of Service or Privacy Policy page and scroll to the bottom where you'll find the delete account option. Note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Why can't I send connection requests?

You may have run out of vibe tokens. Tokens refresh automatically every 30 minutes, or you can purchase more through the app.

How does the bubble system work?

Bubbles show your current activity or mood and help others find you for shared interests. They last for 8 hours and make you appear as an active user.

How do I report inappropriate behavior?

When you match with another user, a report feature becomes available. You can submit a report about inappropriate behavior directly through the app. Include details and screenshots if possible for faster review.
