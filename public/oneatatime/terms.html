What One Is

One is a daily planner for iPhone, iPad, and Mac that helps you tackle your day one task at a time. It helps you:

  • Today: the app always opens with your tasks for Today in a focused list
  • Tomorrow and later: tasks you plan for Tomorrow (or later) are automatically assigned to Today when the next day rolls in
  • Deal mode: focus on your tasks one at a time, with a parallel live activity on your lock screen to help you focus
  • Habits: set your daily and weekly habits, get notified when it's time, and schedule recurring events
  • Synced across your devices: a change on your Mac shows up on your iPhone, and a task you finish on your phone is known on your laptop
  • AI Access (Mac, optional): connect to Claude to analyse your tasks and plan your day, with all data staying local to your Mac
  • Privacy first: your data is stored locally between your iPhone, Mac, and iPad, and One has no servers

One is designed for personal use to help YOU organise YOUR day on YOUR devices.
