Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 30, 2026

🔒

TL;DR

All your data is stored locally between your iPhone, Mac and iPad. One has no servers and can never see your data at any time.

Our Privacy Philosophy

One is a daily planner that helps you tackle your day one task at a time. It is built on a simple principle: your tasks, habits, and notes are yours and yours alone. We do not collect them, we cannot see them, and we never sell or share them.

What One Stores

One stores the tasks, habits, lists, notes, and history you create. This data lives in two places, both controlled by you:

  • On your device, in the app's private storage.
  • In your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database), so it syncs across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

What One Does NOT Do

  • No data collection by the developer - your content is stored only on your devices and in your private iCloud, which only you control.
  • No advertising - there are no ads anywhere in One.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs - no third-party trackers, no cookies.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) - One does not use it.
  • No selling or sharing - we never have your data, so there is nothing to sell or share.

iCloud Sync

Sync is handled entirely by Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) using your private database. Data stored there is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer cannot access data stored in your private iCloud.

AI Access (Optional, on Mac)

One includes an optional "AI Access" feature. When on Mac, you can connect to Claude to analyse your tasks and plan your day.

All data stays local to your Mac and the Claude Code interface. Your data never leaves your Mac through this feature, and it is optional.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, One schedules local notifications on your device, so that when it's time, One reminds you. No reminder content is sent to any server.

Children's Privacy

One does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. Since everything is stored on your device and in your private iCloud, there is no data collection regardless of age.

Data Security

Because your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud, it is protected by Apple's built-in security:

  • Device encryption protecting your storage.
  • Your passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID protecting access to the device.
  • App sandboxing keeping One's data private to One.
  • Apple's iCloud security protecting your private database.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect your data, you already have complete control over it:

  • Access: it is all in the app, on your devices.
  • Delete: delete a task, habit, or list in the app, or delete the app to remove its local data; remove it from iCloud in your iCloud settings.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date above. Privacy-first is not a feature we can switch off; it is how One is built.
