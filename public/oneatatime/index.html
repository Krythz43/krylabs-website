Everything you need to plan your day

Organise your day and the days ahead, and focus on one task at a time.

📋

Today

The app always opens with your tasks for Today in a focused list. No useless dashboards get in the way of the important tasks of your day.

📅

Tomorrow, planned as well

Tasks you plan for Tomorrow are automatically assigned to Today when the next day rolls in. The same happens for tasks you plan for later, so your Today list is filled and well organised at one glance.

🎯

Deal mode

Enter a mode that lets you focus on your tasks one at a time. A parallel live activity starts on your lock screen, outside the app, to help you focus on the same.

🔁

Habits

Your daily and weekly habits are important to you. One notifies you when it's time and also schedules recurring events.

☁️

Synced across all devices

Make a change on your Mac and your iPhone automatically gets the update. Finish a task on your phone and your laptop knows it. You do not have to be device limited for your day to be well planned.

🤖

AI Access

When on Mac, you can optionally connect to Claude to analyse your tasks and plan your day. All data stays local to your Mac and the Claude Code interface.

🔒

Privacy first. No servers.

All your data is stored locally between your iPhone, Mac and iPad. We do not have any servers and can never see your data at any time.
