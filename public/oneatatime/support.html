How do I start my day?

Open Today and enter Deal mode to focus on your tasks one at a time. Swipe left to skip a task for now, or swipe right (or tap Start) to begin it, and a live activity appears on your lock screen to help you focus. Tap Done when you finish.

Does my data sync across devices?

Yes. One syncs your tasks and habits through your private iCloud account. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud and that iCloud is enabled for One on each device.

Do tasks I plan for tomorrow show up automatically?

Yes. Anything scheduled for Tomorrow rolls into Today automatically when the day turns over.

Is my data private?

Yes. One stores your data on your device and in your private iCloud only. See our Privacy Policy for the details.
